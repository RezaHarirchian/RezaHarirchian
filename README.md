## 🧭 About

</div>

I work on **network and systems infrastructure** with a focus on security, availability, and real-world reliability. My background includes designing and operating routing/switching environments, VPN-based remote access, Linux systems, firewalls, and multi-site architectures across on-prem and cloud platforms.

A recurring theme in my work is **how networks behave under non-ideal conditions** — traffic shaping, protocol interference, and large-scale operational constraints. I'm particularly interested in VPN technologies, VoIP/VoLTE behavior, network measurement, and practical resilience techniques grounded in real deployments.

Alongside infrastructure engineering, I bring a background in data-driven digital strategy — connecting low-level architectural decisions with measurable outcomes: **performance, cost, reliability, and user experience**.
